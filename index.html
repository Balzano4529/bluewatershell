Our story

From my heart to the sea.

Hello, I’m Matilda, and I am a pearl diver. I go over the side of our working boat, collect the pearl shells in my bag, and climb back up the ladder with the ocean still sparkling on my cheeks. Those shells become mother of pearl, and I am so very proud to make them into jewellery with my own two hands. From above, the day’s work is a set of long lines in the water. To me, every line is a love letter, written just for you.
